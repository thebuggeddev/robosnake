What the rig can and cannot do

  • 51 skinned bones, all from a humanoid template. No tail chain, no wing bones, no jaw, no neck joint in the skin.
  • Tail is one leaf bone (R_ToeBase) plus the legs. The travelling wave down the coil is not bones at all, it is a rotation applied to the rest positions before skinning.
  • Back blades share Waist and both clavicles with the arms, so they cannot be sprung on their own either. Same trick.
  • Jaw has no bone. It swings around a measured hinge in the vertex shader. It cannot close: the upper fangs interlock into the lower jaw with only 2.5 degrees of clearance, so closing would drive the jaw through them.
  • Joint transforms were missing in the export, so every bone sat at the origin. They were rebuilt from the inverse bind matrices before this page could animate anything.
